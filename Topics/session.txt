A session is a temporary workspace where SQLAlchemy tracks everything you want to do with the database before permanently saving the changes.

Real-life analogy

Imagine you're shopping.
The store is your PostgreSQL database.
The shopping cart is the SQLAlchemy session.

Store (Database)
       │
            ▼
Shopping Cart (Session)
       │
       ├── Add item
       ├── Remove item
       ├── Change quantity
            ▼
Checkout (commit)
